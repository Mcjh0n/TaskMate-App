# Research data separation

Survey answers and System Usability Scale (SUS) responses must be collected outside the TaskMate database, for example with a separately administered form tool. Do not add survey or SUS tables to TaskMate. Do not collect TaskMate account IDs, emails, or other identifiers in the research form, and do not link responses to prototype accounts. Keep research files under the university-approved research storage and retention process. No participant responses or research results are included in this repository.
